Services

Comprehensive assisted living consulting services.

ECC provides practical, Minnesota-focused support across the full assisted living lifecycle — from early feasibility through licensing, documentation, staffing systems, survey preparation, renewal, and ongoing quality management.

From-Scratch Startup Consulting

Business model planning, timeline, budget considerations, license type, capacity strategy, staffing assumptions, service model, and readiness roadmap.

Pre-Property Purchase Review

High-level review of property suitability, safety concerns, resident flow, bedrooms, bathrooms, accessibility, exits, medication storage, kitchen workflow, and operational feasibility.

New Provisional License Application Support

Assisted living license application organization, required attachment planning, operational readiness support, and pre-submission review.

License Renewal Application Support

Renewal timeline tracking, application support, document organization, required updates, and renewal-readiness review.

Policies & Procedures

Policy manuals, procedure workflows, compliance binders, staff-facing procedures, client-ready templates, and implementation tools.

Emergency Preparedness Binder

Emergency preparedness plans, hazard vulnerability assessment, communication plans, drills, tabletop exercises, after-action reports, and CMS Appendix Z-aligned structure where applicable.

Survey Preparation

Mock survey support, chart review checklists, binder organization, staff interview preparation, corrective action planning, and documentation cleanup.

Documentation Forms & Paperwork

MAR/TAR forms, progress notes, incident report forms, refusal logs, service sheets, RN monitoring forms, appointment trackers, cleaning logs, food temperature logs, and audit tools.

Training & Exam Prep

Staff training systems, CFPM test-prep guidance, NAB Core/RCAL exam prep, and Minnesota Assisted Living Director state exam preparation based on 144G and Rule 4659.
