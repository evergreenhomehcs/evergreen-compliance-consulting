What is included in application support?

Support may include readiness review, required document checklist, attachment organization, policy and procedure planning, and guidance on operational readiness before submission.

Can ECC submit the application for me?

This depends on the signed scope of work and client authorization. The client remains responsible for truthful and complete information, fees, and final submission decisions.

No. ECC provides regulatory and operational consulting, not legal advice.
