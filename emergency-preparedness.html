Frequently asked questions

What is included in an emergency preparedness binder?

Typical components include HVA, policies, communication plans, evacuation procedures, drill logs, exercise documentation, emergency contacts, and after-action reports.

Can ECC customize the binder?

Yes. Customization depends on the facility profile, resident needs, staffing model, building layout, and signed scope of work.

Is Appendix Z always required?

Applicability depends on provider type and regulatory context. ECC can align structure to emergency preparedness expectations where applicable.
