Frequently asked questions

Does ECC administer exams?

No. ECC provides study support and navigation guidance. Exams and certifications are administered by authorized organizations or agencies.

Can ECC help with 144G and Rule 4659?

Yes. ECC can create Minnesota-specific study plans, quizzes, and explanations based on assisted living regulatory topics.

Who is exam prep for?

Operators, Assisted Living Director candidates, administrators, managers, and staff preparing for food safety or assisted living regulatory exams.
