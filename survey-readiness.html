No. Survey outcomes cannot be guaranteed. ECC helps strengthen organization, documentation, and readiness.

Can ECC help after correction orders?

Yes. ECC can assist with corrective action planning, documentation organization, staff retraining materials, and implementation tracking depending on scope.
